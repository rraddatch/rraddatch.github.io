Digital accessibility

Radoslav Ďurač

Digital accessibility specialist based in Bratislava and NVDA Certified Expert. I work on the accessibility of websites, mobile applications, non-web documents, and digital products and services in line with WCAG and EN 301 549, including accessible PDF documents under PDF/UA.

About my work

I work on making digital content accessible to all users, including people with disabilities. I focus on compliance with the WCAG and EN 301 549 standards, and on auditing websites and documents.

Areas of expertise

Open source and projects

  • Able Player

    Author of the Slovak translation for the open source accessible media player, and fix for a bug in the getTranslationText() function. The contribution is listed in the changelog for version 5.0.0.

    • PR #747: Slovak translation (merged)
    • PR #748: Add Slovak (sk) language support and fix getTranslationText() bug (merged)
    • Slovak demo

  • Autogram

    Collaboration on the accessibility of the electronic document signing application, its mobile version (Autogram v mobile), and the browser extension used for signing on Slovak government websites.

  • Colour Contrast Analyser (CCA) – Slovak translation

    Author of the Slovak translation of the Colour Contrast Analyser colour-contrast checking tool, which is no longer officially supported. I maintain the translation as a standalone repository, also available as release v3.5.5-sk.1.

  • Luma11y

    Author of the Slovak translation for Luma11y, a tool aiming to replace Colour Contrast Analyser. The translation is submitted as PR #54.
